Account deletion

Delete your My Conveyancing Pro account

You can request deletion of your My Conveyancing Pro account and associated app data without reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your account.
  2. Email Lillynaude.Lilla@gmail.com.
  3. Use the subject My Conveyancing Pro - Account Deletion Request.
  4. Include the account email address you want deleted.

The request will be reviewed and processed. We may verify that the request belongs to the account holder.

What is deleted

Where applicable, deletion covers the account and associated profile, matters, tasks, documents and other app data linked to that account. Limited information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or backup purposes, as described in the Privacy Policy.

In the app

If the app provides an in-app account deletion option, you can also start deletion from your account/settings area.
