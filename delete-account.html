Account deletion

Delete your Lilly's Conveyancing Tracker account

You can delete your account and associated app data yourself, without contacting anyone, or ask by email.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

Sign in at convey-genius.lovable.app, open Settings, then Delete my account. The app shows what will be removed, asks you to type DELETE to confirm, and then closes the account immediately.

Option 2 — by email

Send an email from the address associated with your account:

  • To: Lillynaude.Lilla@gmail.com
  • Subject: Lilly's Conveyancing Tracker - Account Deletion Request
  • Include the account email address you want deleted.

The request will be reviewed and processed. We may verify that the request belongs to the account holder.

What is deleted

Deletion covers the account and its profile, matters, tasks, checklists, important dates, uploaded documents and files, saved colours and activity history. If other people still use the same firm, their matters and files are kept; otherwise the firm record is removed too. Limited information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or backup purposes, as described in the Privacy Policy.
