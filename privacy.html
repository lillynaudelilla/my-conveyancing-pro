Privacy Policy

Lilly's Conveyancing Tracker is a conveyancing matter tracker developed by Lilly Naude. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may store account information such as your email address, name and authentication information; firm information; conveyancing matter information (including transferor and transferee details you choose to record); tasks, checklists and important dates; documents you choose to upload; appearance preferences such as colours and saved colour names; and activity or audit information associated with use of the app.

How information is used

This information is used to authenticate you, provide matter and task-management features, store and retrieve documents you choose to upload, remind you of due dates and expiries, maintain security and access controls, and respond to support requests.

Where data is stored

The app uses Supabase for authentication, database storage and file storage. Documents are held in a private store that is only reachable by signed-in members of your own firm. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Supabase may process data on behalf of the app as its service provider. The app does not sell your personal information.

Document reading (AI feature)

If you use the "Read deed of sale" feature, the PDF you choose to upload is sent to a third-party document-reading service (OpenAI, or the app's AI hosting provider) so that details such as the parties, property description, price and stated deadlines can be extracted and shown to you for review. Nothing is created in the app until you confirm it. Extracted details are then saved with your matter in your own database. Please review the AI provider's privacy policy before uploading documents that contain information you are not authorised to share.

Sharing

Information is not sold or shared for advertising. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, such as those named above, and may be disclosed where required by law.

Documents and sensitive information

You are responsible for deciding what information and documents you upload. Because conveyancing records can contain sensitive information, only upload information you are authorised to store and use in the app.

Retention and deletion

Account and associated app data are retained while your account is active. You can delete your account and your data yourself from Settings > Delete my account inside the app, or request deletion through the public account deletion page. When an account is deleted, the account, its matters, tasks, dates, documents and uploaded files are removed. Limited information may be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or technical backup processes.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information, including authenticated access, per-firm database access controls, an optional device PIN lock and encrypted network connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Lilly's Conveyancing Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.
