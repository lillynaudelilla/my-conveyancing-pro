Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

My Conveyancing Pro is a personal productivity application developed by Lilly Naude. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may store account information such as your email address, name and authentication information; firm information; conveyancing matter information; tasks and checklists; documents you choose to upload; and activity or audit information associated with use of the app.

How information is used

This information is used to authenticate you, provide matter and task-management features, store and retrieve documents you choose to upload, maintain security and access controls, and respond to support requests.

Where data is stored

The app uses Supabase for authentication, database storage and file storage. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Supabase may process data on behalf of the app as its service provider. The app does not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Information is not sold or shared for advertising. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, such as Supabase, and may be disclosed where required by law.

Documents and sensitive information

You are responsible for deciding what information and documents you upload. Because conveyancing records can contain sensitive information, only upload information you are authorised to store and use in the app.

Retention and deletion

Account and associated app data are retained while your account is active. You may request account deletion through the app or through the public account deletion page. When an account is deleted, associated app data is intended to be deleted, subject to limited retention required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or technical backup processes.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information, including authenticated access, database access controls and encrypted network connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

My Conveyancing Pro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
